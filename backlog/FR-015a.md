## User Story

**As a** Product Owner,
**I want** to know in which categories, brands and customer segments the size recommendation is most often wrong,
**so that** we invest model improvements where they reduce the most returns.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1 – Accuracy baseline
- **Given** order and return data of the last 3 months
- **When** the analysis is done
- **Then** recommendation accuracy (kept items / items ordered in the recommended size) is reported per category, brand and data situation (history-based vs. fit-profile-based)

### AC2 – Root causes and follow-up
- **Given** the analysis results
- **When** the spike is closed
- **Then** the top 3 root causes are documented with an estimated impact on the return rate and a proposal for follow-up stories

## Notes
- Time-box: 3 days. The spike ends after 3 days even if not all questions are answered; open points are documented.
- Success criterion for any follow-up work: KPI "recommendation accuracy ≥ 80 %" (see [main README](../README.md#product-goal--kpis)).
- **No follow-up story (FR-015b) is created yet** – its scope, segment and baseline depend on the spike result (PO decision 2026-11-12, see [decision log](../docs/po-decision-log.md)).
