# FR-003: Review and approve fit hints before publishing

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **ID** | FR-003 |
| **Release** | R1 – Fit Insights |
| **Priority** | Must have |
| **Story Points** | 5 |
| **Status** | Ready |

## User Story

**As a** Category Manager,
**I want** to review AI-generated fit hints for my category before they go live,
**so that** customers only see hints that I consider correct and that do not damage the relationship with our suppliers.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1 – Review list
- **Given** I am logged in to the backoffice with the role "Category Manager"
- **When** I open the "Fit Hints" view
- **Then** I see all new fit hints for my categories with article, brand, proposed hint, number of underlying returns and distribution of fit categories

### AC2 – Approve
- **Given** a proposed fit hint is in status "pending"
- **When** I click "Approve"
- **Then** the status changes to "approved" and the hint is shown on the product page within 15 minutes

### AC3 – Reject with reason
- **Given** a proposed fit hint is in status "pending"
- **When** I click "Reject" and select a reason (e.g. "data issue", "article will be changed by supplier")
- **Then** the hint is not published and the rejection reason is stored

### AC4 – Edit
- **Given** a proposed fit hint is in status "pending"
- **When** I change the hint to one of the predefined alternative texts and approve it
- **Then** the edited hint is published and the change is logged with my user name and timestamp

### AC5 – Notification
- **Given** new fit hints have been generated for my categories
- **When** the daily generation job has finished
- **Then** I receive one summary e-mail with the number of pending hints and a link to the review list

## Out of Scope
- Free-text editing of hints (only predefined texts in R1)
