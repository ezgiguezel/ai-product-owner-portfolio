# FR-005: Track fit hint impact with an A/B test

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **ID** | FR-005 |
| **Release** | R1 – Fit Insights |
| **Priority** | Must have |
| **Story Points** | 3 |
| **Status** | Ready |

## User Story

**As a** Product Owner,
**I want** fit hints to be rolled out as an A/B test with tracking of views, orders and returns,
**so that** I can prove whether FitRight reduces the return rate without lowering conversion.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1 – Group assignment
- **Given** a visitor opens a product page for the first time
- **When** the A/B test is active
- **Then** the visitor is randomly and persistently assigned to group A (no fit hint) or group B (fit hint) in a 50/50 split

### AC2 – Event tracking
- **Given** a visitor in group B sees a fit hint
- **When** the hint becomes visible in the viewport
- **Then** an event `fit_hint_viewed` with article ID, hint type and test group is sent to the analytics system

### AC3 – Order and return attribution
- **Given** an order contains articles with a fit hint
- **When** the order and any later returns are processed
- **Then** order lines and returns can be attributed to the visitor's test group in the reporting

### AC4 – Consent
- **Given** a visitor has not given analytics consent
- **When** they open a product page
- **Then** no tracking events are sent and the visitor is assigned to group A

## Notes
- Minimum test duration: 6 weeks (returns arrive with a delay of up to 30 days).
