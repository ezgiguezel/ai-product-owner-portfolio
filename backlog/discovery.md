# FitRight – Discovery Backlog

This list holds **solution ideas that are not ready for delivery**. They need validation (customer value, feasibility, data availability, legal/GDPR) before they can become user stories. Items here are **not estimated** and **not planned** in a release.

An idea moves into the product backlog only when the key questions are answered and the idea can be linked to a FitRight KPI (see [Definition of Ready](../docs/definition-of-ready.md)).

| ID | Idea | Origin | Key questions to validate | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | **Body scan via smartphone camera** for guests and new customers | [FR-008](FR-008.md) | Do customers trust and accept a camera-based scan? Accuracy vs. the fit profile questionnaire (FR-009)? GDPR: processing of body images / biometric data, consent, storage? Build vs. buy (third-party providers)? | Open |
| D-02 | **AR try-on** | [FR-008](FR-008.md) | Does AR try-on reduce size-related returns, or mainly help with style/look? Device coverage, effort per article (3D assets)? | Open |
| D-03 | **Style preferences** as recommendation input | [FR-008](FR-008.md) | Does style influence size choice (e.g. oversized look) enough to change the recommendation? How do we capture preferences without extra friction? | Open |
| D-04 | **Weather** as recommendation input | [FR-008](FR-008.md) | Is there any plausible link between weather and size-related returns? Likely more relevant for product recommendations than for size. | Open |
| D-05 | **Forecast of future returns** for Category Management | [FR-013](FR-013.md) | Which decisions would a forecast change (buying, stock, supplier talks)? Is enough history available per article/season? | Open |
| D-06 | **Comparison with competitors** in the return insights | [FR-013](FR-013.md) | Is there any reliable data source for competitor return rates (benchmarks, panels)? Cost and legal constraints? | Open |
| D-07 | **CO₂ impact of returns** | [FR-013](FR-013.md) | Which emission factors (transport, packaging, disposal) can we use? Owner: Sustainability team? Who would act on this view? | Open |
