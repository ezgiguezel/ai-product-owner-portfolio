# FR-002: Show fit hint on the product detail page

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **ID** | FR-002 |
| **Release** | R1 – Fit Insights |
| **Priority** | Must have |
| **Story Points** | 5 |
| **Status** | Ready |

## User Story

**As a** customer browsing a product page,
**I want** to see a short hint on how the item fits (e.g. "Runs small – most customers recommend one size up"),
**so that** I can choose the right size before ordering and avoid a return.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1 – Hint for articles running small or large
- **Given** an article has at least 30 classified size-related returns
- **And** at least 60 % of them are "too small" (or "too large")
- **When** I open the product detail page
- **Then** I see the fit hint "Runs small – we recommend one size up" (or "Runs large – we recommend one size down") directly above the size selector

### AC2 – True to size
- **Given** an article has at least 30 classified size-related returns
- **And** no fit category exceeds 60 %
- **When** I open the product detail page
- **Then** I see the hint "True to size"

### AC3 – Not enough data
- **Given** an article has fewer than 30 classified size-related returns
- **When** I open the product detail page
- **Then** no fit hint is shown and the page layout does not leave an empty gap

### AC4 – Only approved hints
- **Given** a fit hint has not been approved by Category Management (see FR-003)
- **When** I open the product detail page
- **Then** the hint is not displayed

### AC5 – Mobile
- **Given** I use the shop on a mobile device (viewport ≤ 400 px)
- **When** I open the product detail page
- **Then** the fit hint is fully readable without horizontal scrolling

## Dependencies
- FR-001 (classification), FR-003 (approval workflow)
