## User Story

**As a** logged-in customer buying shoes,
**I want** to see a personal shoe size recommendation on shoe product pages,
**so that** I order the right shoe size the first time and do not need to return the shoes because they are too small or too large.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1 – Recommendation for shoes
- **Given** I am logged in, have given consent (FR-011) and kept at least 2 pairs of shoes in the last 24 months
- **When** I open a shoe product page
- **Then** I see "Your recommended size: [size]" next to the size selector, as in FR-006

### AC2 – Shoe size systems
- **Given** the article uses a specific shoe size system (e.g. EU, UK or US, including half sizes)
- **When** the recommendation is calculated
- **Then** the recommended size is shown in the article's own size system

### AC3 – Length only
- **Given** the article is offered in several widths
- **When** the recommendation is shown
- **Then** it refers to the shoe length only and shows the note "Recommendation refers to length only"
- **And** no width recommendation is given (shoe width is out of scope for this story)

### AC4 – Quality gate before go-live
- **Given** a backtest on shoe orders of the last 12 months
- **When** the model is evaluated
- **Then** at least 80 % of historical pairs ordered in the recommended size were kept (KPI "recommendation accuracy")

### AC5 – Fallback
- **Given** I do not meet the data or consent requirements from AC1
- **When** I open a shoe product page
- **Then** the same fallback behavior as in FR-006 AC5 applies

## Dependencies
- FR-006 (personal size recommendation, incl. fallback AC5), FR-011 (consent)

## Notes
- Shoe width recommendation remains in the remaining scope of the epic [FR-008](FR-008.md).
