# FR-009: Fit profile for new customers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **ID** | FR-009 |
| **Release** | R2 – My Size |
| **Priority** | Should have |
| **Story Points** | 5 |
| **Status** | Refined |

## User Story

**As a** new customer without order history,
**I want** to enter a few details about my body and fit preferences,
**so that** I also receive a size recommendation for my first order.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1 – Entry point
- **Given** I have no order history and no fit profile
- **When** I open a product page in a supported category
- **Then** I see the link "Find your size in 30 seconds"

### AC2 – Short questionnaire
- **Given** I opened the fit profile questionnaire
- **When** I enter height, weight, usual size in one known brand and my fit preference ("tight", "regular", "loose")
- **Then** I see a size recommendation for the current product immediately after submitting

### AC3 – Optional fields and validation
- **Given** I am filling in the questionnaire
- **When** I enter a height outside 140–210 cm or a weight outside 35–200 kg
- **Then** I see an inline error message and cannot submit
- **And** all fields except height and usual size are optional

### AC4 – Save profile
- **Given** I am logged in and complete the questionnaire
- **And** I have given explicit consent to store my body data
- **When** I submit the questionnaire
- **Then** the fit profile is saved to my account and used for future recommendations

### AC5 – Guest usage
- **Given** I am not logged in
- **When** I complete the questionnaire
- **Then** my data is only used for the current session and is not stored after the session ends
